Security, privacy, and regulatory compliance must be embedded throughout the lifecycle, not treated as afterthoughts.

* Security requirements must be identified during planning and design
* Secure coding and testing practices must be followed
* Solutions must comply with organisational and regulatory standards
* Sensitive data must be identified, classified, and protected at every stage of the lifecycle
* Data Loss Prevention (DLP) controls must be embedded into development, testing, and operational practices — see Section 16
* Privacy and data protection obligations (including GDPR and other applicable regulations) must be considered from the earliest stages of discovery
