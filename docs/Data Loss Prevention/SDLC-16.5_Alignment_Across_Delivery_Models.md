### **16.5 Alignment Across Delivery Models**

* **Agile / Product Models**:

  * Data classification and DLP requirements should be identified during discovery and treated as non-functional requirements on the backlog
  * DLP checks (secret scanning, SAST, access reviews) should be embedded in CI/CD pipelines and automated quality gates
  * Iterative delivery does not reduce DLP obligations — controls must be in place before sensitive data enters any environment

* **Waterfall**:

  * Data classification and PIA activities should be completed during the Design phase
  * Formal reviews of data handling controls should be conducted at relevant stage gates
  * Access controls and environment data standards must be documented and approved before build begins
