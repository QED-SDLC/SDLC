
### **16.1 Objectives**

The objectives of DLP are to:

* Prevent accidental or malicious data leakage during development, testing, and production operation
* Ensure compliance with applicable privacy regulations, industry standards, and contractual obligations
* Identify and protect sensitive data in code, documentation, configurations, repositories, and systems
* Embed data protection controls into SDLC practices, tooling, and automation
* Provide monitoring, alerting, and response mechanisms for potential data loss events
* Ensure accountability for data protection is clearly assigned throughout delivery
