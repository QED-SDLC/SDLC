## **16. Data Loss Prevention (DLP)**

Data Loss Prevention defines the policies, practices, and controls used to prevent, detect, and respond to the unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss of sensitive information throughout the software development lifecycle. DLP protects both organisational assets and the data of customers, partners, and employees.

DLP is not a standalone concern — it is embedded across every phase of the SDLC, from discovery and design through to build, test, release, and operation. This section defines the minimum expectations and controls that apply to all initiatives handling sensitive data.

Data Loss Prevention ensures that sensitive information is **protected, monitored, and controlled** throughout the software lifecycle. By embedding DLP into every phase, the organisation mitigates risk, maintains compliance, and builds trust with stakeholders and customers.
